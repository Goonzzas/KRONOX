# Planet texture credits

The equirectangular surface maps in this directory are from the Solar System
Scope texture pack and are distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0).

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Files used:

- `2k_mercury.jpg` — Mercury
- `2k_venus.jpg` and `2k_venus_atmosphere.jpg` — Venus surface and cloud deck
- `2k_earth_daymap.jpg`, `2k_earth_clouds.jpg`, and `2k_earth_normal_map.jpg` — Earth
- `2k_moon.jpg` — Moon
- `2k_mars.jpg` — Mars
- `2k_jupiter.jpg` — Jupiter
- `2k_saturn.jpg` — Saturn
- `2k_uranus.jpg` — Uranus
- `2k_neptune.jpg` — Neptune

The source notes that some unmapped regions are filled with representative
terrain, the colors are tuned for visibility, and the gas giant maps are
representative rather than time-dependent. These images are for visualization,
not scientific analysis.
